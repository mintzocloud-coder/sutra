# Contributor License Agreement (CLA) — Sutra

> Sign once before your first merged PR. Two minutes. This is the standard Harmony-style contributor agreement.

## What you're agreeing to

1. **You keep your copyright.** Your contributions remain yours.
2. **You grant the project a permanent license** to your contributions — to use, modify, distribute, and sublicense them — including the right to **relicense** the project (e.g., offer commercial licenses alongside AGPLv3).
3. **Your contributions are original** — your own work, or work you have the right to contribute. No copied proprietary code.
4. **Patent grant** — you grant a royalty-free patent license for patents necessarily infringed by your contributions.

## Why this exists

AGPLv3 keeps the public code free forever. The CLA lets the project also offer **commercial licenses** (for companies that can't use AGPLv3) without tracking down every contributor years later. One signature now, no friction ever.

## How to sign

- Comment **"I agree to the CLA"** on your first PR, **or**
- Sign via the CLA bot when it prompts you (coming soon).

No signature → no merge. This is enforced, not optional.
